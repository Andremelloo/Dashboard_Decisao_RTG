# 🏗️ RTG Fleet Strategic Decision Dashboard
### Paranaguá Container Terminal (TCP) • China Merchants Port (CMPort)

Interactive Executive Decision Dashboard for the strategic replacement vs retrofit evaluation of TCP's RTG fleet (RTGs 01 to 41, focus on bays 04 to 14, preservation of 01 to 03, and electrification of 11 to 14).

## 🌐 Live Web Version
Access the online dashboard: **[https://andremelloo.github.io/Dashboard_Decisao_RTG/](https://andremelloo.github.io/Dashboard_Decisao_RTG/)**

## 🚀 Key Features
- **Trilingual Interface:** Instant language switching across **🇬🇧 English (Default)**, **🇧🇷 Portuguese (Brazil)**, and **🇭🇰 Traditional Chinese (CMPort standard)**.
- **Parametric Financial Simulator:** Dynamic LCC (Life Cycle Cost) & sensitivity testing comparing 3 strategic board solutions.
- **Real ZPMC 2024 Benchmark:** 2.8 years of audited data showing real operational cost of **US$ 4.16/h** (R$ 22.90/h) and 671 h/month utilization.
- **Kayo Subsystems Matrix (Rev 1.0):** Complete 11 RTGs x 10 mandatory CMPort subsystems audited with real maintenance failure records.
- **Maritime Shipping Synergies:** Combined chartering of 10 new E-RTGs with 2 STSs on a single heavy-lift vessel (US$ 4.0M freight savings).
